I’m Šimon, a freelance software engineer based in Prague. Outside work, music and climbing are my main pursuits: I play guitar, sing, and climb indoors and outdoors.

Photography, videography, editing, cinematography, and short indie filmmaking are new interests I’m only beginning to explore. I don’t own dedicated camera gear yet; for now, I’m learning with my iPhone 11 and the Blackmagic Camera app. The gallery is a small set of photos I’ve made with that setup, and I’m learning as I go.

This site brings together my work, projects, interests, and whatever else I’m thinking about. For a quick professional overview, take a look at [my CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}).
